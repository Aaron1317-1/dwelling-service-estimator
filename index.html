Conductor distinctions and limits

The GEC output is the service grounding electrode conductor from the lecture Table 5.8 / NEC Table 250.66. It is not the equipment grounding conductor (EGC); downstream EGCs are sized from their OCPD under Table 250.122. Service bonding jumpers are a separate calculation. Single-phase phase conductors follow lecture Table 7.5 for 100–400 A; three-phase phase conductors use a 75°C ampacity reference. Services beyond the included tables require manual review. Estimates do not apply every ampacity adjustment or correction.

Main breaker is the next listed standard rating at or above calculated current, with a 100 A one-family dwelling service-disconnect assumption (230.79(C), 240.6). Check complete installation and local adoption.
